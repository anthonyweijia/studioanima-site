The world model

The highest human creativity, unleashed by the most advanced technology.

Generative AI predicts what tends to follow, not what relationships must hold. That is why notes, reference images, and careful instructions only make consistency and correctness likely, not certain. Our world model is different in kind: it learns your world — the cast, the light, the story, the rules — holds it as living state, and derives what every fact implies. It composes what has never existed — new scenes, new variants, whole new films. And every composition is correct, faithful to the laws of reality and to the smallest facts of your story. In practice: a new idea is fully produced in a fraction of the time and cost it previously took to begin exploring it; a thousand variants remain one world; and craft compounds with speed.
